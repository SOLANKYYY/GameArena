## The Games

1. **Dice**: Set your win chance. Roll underneath or over the target number to win the associated multiplier.
2. **Keno**: Pick your lucky numbers from the grid! The more numbers you match from the drawn sequence, the higher your payout.
3. **Limbo**: Set a target payout multiplier. If the randomly generated multiplier is higher than your target, you win your target multiplier!
4. **Mines**: Uncover gems to increase your current multiplier, but don't hit a mine! You can "Cash Out" at any time to secure your winnings.
5. **Moles**: A game of fast reactions! Whack the moles to build your multiplier before time runs out or you hit a dud.
6. **Plinko**: Drop the ball into the peg maze. Your payout is determined by the multiplier bucket the ball lands in at the bottom.
7. **Wheel**: Spin the wheel of fortune. Choose your risk segment and hope the flapper lands on a high multiplier!
